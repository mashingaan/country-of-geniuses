# Country of Geniuses logo, concept v1

Created with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-08-27.

## Deliverables

- country-of-geniuses-logo-v1.png: primary logo with the exact brand name
- country-of-geniuses-avatar-v1.png: standalone monogram for a square avatar

Both final images use a white background. They are raster PNG artwork, not vector masters. The first generated draft was rejected because its circular symbol resembled an existing logo and its simulated transparency was not an alpha channel. The selected direction is the geometric C/G monogram.

## Initial exploration prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: primary logo for an open-source civic technology project, usable in a GitHub README and on a website.
Brand name, exact text: "Country of Geniuses"

Create one polished, distinctive logo, not a sheet of alternatives or a mockup.
Context: an international open-source community shares agent skills and government API integrations. Participants use their own AI agents to find public-service problems, verify evidence and help route them to the right services. The brand should feel intelligent, open, humane, credible and collaborative.

Design a compact abstract symbol suggesting an open civic forum, assembled from a small number of bold modular geometric forms. Use intentional negative space and an open boundary to convey participation and distributed cooperation. Aim for a memorable silhouette that still works as a small GitHub avatar. Choose one clear visual idea and resolve it beautifully. Avoid a complex network diagram.

Place the symbol beside a carefully typeset wordmark reading exactly "Country of Geniuses". The wordmark may occupy two well-balanced lines. Use contemporary, timeless, highly legible typography with precise spacing. The symbol and typography should feel like one identity.

Flat, crisp, vector-like graphic design. One-color dark ink artwork on a genuinely transparent background. Generous clear space around the entire lockup. No gradients, shadows, texture, photography, 3D, border or watermark. No extra text, slogan, initials outside the symbol, presentation labels or color samples. Avoid literal brains, lightbulbs, robots, AI sparkles, national flags, crowns, official government seals and recognizable existing logos. The result should be the actual usable logo artwork.
```

## Selected logo refinement prompt

The first generated draft was used as the edit input.

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Edit the supplied Country of Geniuses logo.

Preserve the exact wordmark text "Country of Geniuses", its attractive two-line arrangement, the dark ink color, the contemporary bold typography, and the generous surrounding space.

Replace the entire symbol on the left with a completely different original geometric C/G monogram. The new symbol should have a compact almost-square silhouette and use two broad, carefully proportioned bracket-like forms with gently rounded corners. An outer open C shape and a smaller inward G bar should share deliberate negative space, suggesting an open gateway and a shared civic workspace. Strong balanced proportions, a distinctive cut or opening, no fine details. It must be legible as a standalone avatar.

The old circular ring of three people must be completely removed. Do not use dots as heads, human figures around a circle, a three-part ring, or any Ubuntu-like motif. Avoid a literal government building, shield, globe, brain, lightbulb or AI sparkle.

Replace the entire checkerboard background with uniform pure white. The background must be completely flat white, with no pattern, texture, shadows or simulated transparency. Dark solid artwork, no gradients. Match the visual weight of the new monogram to the wordmark. Deliver a single actual logo lockup with immaculate edges and precise spacing, not a presentation board or mockup.
```
