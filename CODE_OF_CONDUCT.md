# Code of Conduct

We want a practical, welcoming community that can disagree about evidence and public systems without attacking people.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and specific.
- Separate claims from evidence and uncertainty.
- Welcome local context and corrections.
- Protect personal data and the safety of people mentioned in cases.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, doxxing, spam, and coordinated pressure campaigns.

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove material that creates a privacy, safety, or abuse risk. Please report concerns privately through the channel described in `SECURITY.md`.
